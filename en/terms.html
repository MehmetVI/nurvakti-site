Using the app

NurVakti provides prayer times, sourced Quran content, mosque information and personal reading tools. You do not need to open an account. Times can differ depending on the selected location, calculation method and data source; check them against your local official calendar. Calculated times are not presented as official institutional data. Information labeled DEMO is an example. Whether notifications arrive depends on device permissions and operating-system settings.

Digital Pro products and price

The NurVakti Pro (Premium) products offered on the purchase screen give access to digital features. Before buying, check the product’s scope and period and the total price in the current local currency shown by the store. Monthly and yearly subscriptions

The monthly subscription renews automatically for one-month periods and the yearly subscription for one-year periods. To stop renewal, cancel in the subscription settings of the store where you bought it, before the renewal date shown by the store. Cancelling normally does not remove the remaining time of a paid period; the store’s refund or entitlement-revocation actions may change access. Removing the app or deleting data on your device does not cancel the subscription.

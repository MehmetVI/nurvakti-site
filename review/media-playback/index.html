NurVakti: background audio playback

Google Play foreground service declaration: FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK.

The recording shows the user starting Quran recitation from the listening screen, then the Android media notification displaying ongoing playback while the application is in the background.

  1. Open NurVakti → Kur’an → Dinle ve indir (Listen and download).
  2. Select a surah and press Seçimle dinlemeyi başlat (Start listening).
  3. Leave the app and open the Android notification shade to observe continued playback.
